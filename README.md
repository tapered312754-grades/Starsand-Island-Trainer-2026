## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Starsand Island**. This trainer enhances the island-life experience by offering optional controls for movement, resources, inventory management, exploration, and combat. Use the features to reduce repetitive tasks, experiment with farming and crafting, or explore Starsand Island at your own pace.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce repetitive farming and crafting tasks, explore the island more freely, experiment with building and resources, or make combat and daily activities more relaxed.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game normally, load a save, and wait until the player can move freely before activating movement features. Use the speed multiplier for faster travel on foot and disable it before entering menus, vehicles, scripted scenes, or multiplayer sessions.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory and select the item you want to modify before using the item quantity feature. Change only reasonable amounts, then close and reopen the inventory to confirm the result. Avoid editing quest items or unique story objects.

### World & Environment

Use world features after loading into Starsand Island or an accessible exploration area. Freeze time when you need additional time for farming, fishing, building, social quests, or resource gathering. Save normally after completing an activity and disable time-related options before transitioning between areas.

### Combat & Weapons

Enter a combat area before enabling health or damage protection features. These options are intended for encounters with hostile creatures during exploration. Disable combat options before story events, cutscenes, or multiplayer activities to reduce the chance of progression or synchronization issues.
